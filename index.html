A private space for daily reflection

Your journey is yours.

A quiet place for Stoic reflection, gratitude, and intentional practice. No streaks to impress. No pressure to perform.

AKRATĒS is an Android app for personal journaling, daily check-ins and self-directed practices. Journal records are kept on the device, with optional Google-account connection for Google Drive backup and restoration.

AKRATĒS is under development. This is its official public information and support page.

The essentials

Privacy matters. The app stores private notes locally in protected storage. If Google backup is enabled, copies are stored in the Google Drive app-specific hidden area. These cloud copies are not end-to-end encrypted.
